Featured · LLM + Semantic Search

InternPact

AI Recommender

An AI internship recommender. Upload a CV and an LLM turns it into a structured profile, then the app ranks live internship offers against that profile and shows exactly which skills you're missing and how to learn them.

  • LLM CV parsing (Claude, with Gemini and rule-based fallbacks) of PDF, DOCX or text CVs into skills, education and experience
  • Live internship offers pulled from the JSearch API, ranked with a hybrid candidate–job score and explained with a skill-gap analysis and personalised learning path
  • Compared four matching strategies (keyword matching, TF-IDF, sentence-transformer embeddings, LLM reasoning) using Precision@5, Recall@5 and NDCG@5

Python, FastAPI, Claude API, sentence-transformers, FAISS, spaCy, React, Vite

Featured · Machine Learning

Heart Disease Prediction

ML App

An end-to-end machine learning app that predicts heart disease risk from clinical data. A logistic regression model is served through a Flask API, and each prediction comes with a confidence score.

77%accuracy
90%recall on positive cases
  • Found and removed 723 duplicate rows (70.5% of the 1,025-row UCI dataset). Duplicates leak between train and test sets and push typical results on this dataset to near-perfect accuracy, so 77% is an honest score.
  • Upload a CSV, explore it in an interactive Chart.js dashboard, and get a risk prediction for a single patient

Educational project, not medical advice.

Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Flask, JavaScript, Chart.js
